# CeyFi App

Financial management for people and businesses in Sri Lanka.

A clearer picture of your money, built for everyday life in Sri Lanka.

🌐 **Website:** https://ceyfi.app

## What we build

- **Personal** — everyday money management for individuals
- **Business** — money management for businesses
- **Orgs** — money management for organisations

## Repositories

- [ceyfi-website](https://github.com/CeyFiApp/ceyfi-website) — the CeyFi website and web app, built with Next.js 16 and deployed on Cloudflare Workers
- [ceyfi-android](https://github.com/CeyFiApp/ceyfi-android) — the CeyFi Android app, built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose
